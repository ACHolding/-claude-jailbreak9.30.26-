FableSDK 0.1 is an AI development toolkit inspired by the Fable 5 system card’s emphasis on highly capable reasoning, coding, autonomous work, and advanced research. It incorporates a safety-oriented architecture designed to preserve useful capabilities while adding safeguards around higher-risk areas such as cybersecurity, biological research, and frontier AI development. FableSDK 0.1 is designed around the system card’s core idea of combining frontier-level capability with stronger controls for broad deployment.
