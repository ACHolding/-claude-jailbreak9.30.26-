CatSDK Fable/Opus is a collaborative development system for creating security-focused tools, complete game engines, interactive simulations, and story-driven experiences. It turns ideas into working projects by combining programming, debugging, testing, world-building, and defensive security workflows while keeping real-world security activity authorized and controlled.
